Nearest game: Rapala Pro Bass Fishing (2010); this one differs in a stop-the-lure pull gauge on a straight line (zones move every stop, pulls add up and a miss takes one back) in place of a line-tension fight, strain tiers that decide which rarities bite, shade that moves with the sun as the sweet spot, and a Hunger/Munchies bar that limits getting higher. The gauge takes green +1 and blue +3 from the BioShock 2 hack the owner named, not its dial, its stages or its alarm.
