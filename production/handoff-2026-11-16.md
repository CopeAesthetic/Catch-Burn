## 1. What the game is

A first-person, retro (PS1–PS2 look) fishing collect-a-thon with stoner comedy and drop-in co-op. Core loop: **catch fish → sell fish → buy weed → smoke weed → unlock rarer fish → unlock better weed → repeat**, across 8 regions, 32 ponds, 320 fish, 128 strains. Solo developer, goal: sell on Steam. 18+.

## 5. Owner decisions (all are in the brief; this is the index)

**Rules**
- Lost Legends are photographed and released, not sold. Reward: Straight Fire unlock + trophy photo in Unc's log.
- An area's 4 Lost Legends open when that area's 36 other fish are caught (replaces "after all 288"). They gate nothing: a victory lap.
- YAK LOCK needs one extra hit at 100%. YAK LOCKED lasts 60 s and makes fishing harder. Under Top Shelf it gives a guaranteed 50/50 Epic-or-Legendary.
- Chill bands: Chill 1–24, High 25–49, Stoned 50–74, Zonked 75–100.
- A tier opens every rarity up to its own, weighted; a higher Chill state improves the odds.
- A strain from another pond gives visuals only, no rarity (except the Lost Legend rule).
- Straight Fire is the victory-lap strain: every rarity, boosted odds and value.
- Smoking a second strain: the last one smoked takes over; the meter carries on.
- Selling: fish go in The Drop Box, Jimmy pays overnight. Quick Sell is instant at about 60%.
- Appraise: true value hidden; unappraised sells at a flat price per rarity.
- You fish until midnight, then drift off and wake in bed. Fish not in The Drop Box when you sleep spoil → Quick Sell price only.
- A day lasts about 15–20 minutes (starting value).
- Blackout (10 beers): lose carried fish and Cooler contents only.
- Each shack has a Map: shows the area's 4 ponds as pictures. All 9 non-Lost-Legend species of a pond must be caught to **buy** the next pond's picture. All 36 in an area → next area ticket from Joe.
- Joe handles areas; the Map handles ponds.
- Storage: fridge (food, unlimited), dresser (weed + cosmetics, unlimited), Cooler (the on-water limit, upgradable), stringer (fish carried, limited, upgradable).
- Tackle Box: five slots — rod, line, lure, smoking item, mask.
- Starting kit: Grandpa's Cane Pole, Lunchbox cooler, Rental Kayak, One Hitter, $0.
- Flip phone: mouse and keys; the world keeps running while it is open.
- Bite waits (5 s open, 2 s shade) are averages with about ±1 s jitter. Hook-set window 1.5 s.
- Cosmetics: clothes, kayak and rod skins, shack decorations, smoking gear skins. **Earned by fishing, never bought.**

**Fish Database doc**
- Each pond's fish is its own log entry (320 entries; 134 distinct species + 32 Lost Legends to draw).
- Catches roll a weight; a catch in the top 5% of its range is a trophy (by weight alone, about 1 in 32).
- Junk: a shared list plus regional junk. 40% of sober casts once Commons are logged, capped at 80%.
- An unlogged fish is twice as likely within its rarity.
- Starting odds are the sketch's (Legendary 10% under Top Shelf at Chill, 15% Zonked).

**Naming**
- Tribute names kept, each with a true Unc story: Jack Herring (Jack Herer), Jenny Kush Jig, Les Anderson's King.
- Gary Payton Pole → Super Boof Spoonbill. Eleven brand-derived names replaced (rename log in the content file).

**Pillars** (in the concept doc)
1. The High Is the Progression — which fish you can hook comes from the strain, never money or gear.
2. Chill First, Never Punishing.
3. Real Fish, Real Places, Pun Names.
4. Budget Charm.
5. Better With Friends, Whole Alone.
Anti-pillars: not a simulation, not competitive, not a growing/crafting game, not mean or edgy.

**Art bible**
- Visual rule: "The world is a calm, misty pond; the high is what happens to it. Keep the base world quiet and low-fi, and let colour, never clutter, carry the joke."
- Vibrance and blur touch the 3D world only; the HUD never changes. Shade is a brightness gap (about 3:1), not a hue.
- Pink means Lost Legend only. YAK LOCKED meter is lime-and-white stripes. Money is cream; Hunger bar tan; gold is Legendary only.

**MVP**
- 19 systems; includes a stand-in order screen at the dock (no Flip Phone until the Vertical Slice).
- Slice adjustments: start with a One Hitter; Silver Trout and Silver Smoke behind a debug toggle.
